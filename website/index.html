Protocol

How Therdal works

Four simple steps across independent roles—voters just sign their choice.

1
Topic Creator Create a Topic Set question & choices
2
ETH Holders Cast Your Vote Free offline or direct
3
Vote Aggregator Bundle Votes Collect & post batches
4
Batch Prover Verify Results Prove & finalize tally
Step 1 Topic Creator

Create a Topic & Set the Choices

Ask a question and define custom voting options. Creation snapshots Ethereum block headers at that moment—moving or buying ETH later has no effect, and ETH is never locked.

Custom Choices Block Header Snapshot ETH Never Locked
Step 2 ETH Holders

Cast Your Vote—Offline or Direct

Pick an option using your wallet and staked ETH. Sign offline for $0 gas—keeping cold wallets safe—or vote directly on Ethereum. Once you vote, you're done.

Done in One Step Wallets & Validators Cold Wallets Stay Safe
Step 3 Vote Aggregator

Bundle Votes Together

Aggregators host voting frontends, collect offline signatures, and post thousands together on Ethereum in one batch. Anyone can run one, preventing censorship.

Permissionless No Single Gatekeeper Publicly Auditable
Step 4 Batch Prover

Verify & Finalize the Tally

Provers generate a cryptographic proof checking signatures, snapshot ETH balances, and uniqueness. Ethereum verifies the proof in one step and updates the public tally.

Math, Not Trust Zero Double Votes On-Chain Tally
